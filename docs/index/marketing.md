# Marketing & Social Media

### For the game devs that hate social media:
https://youtu.be/iHuUNbudQVc?t=1676 Cakez - Marketing Can Be So Simple - Viral Indie Dev Explains 27:56

- If you have an unique enough idea, you can just record your screen, upload to Tiktok and reupload to Instagram and Youtube. The watermark spreads itself.
- If the idea is unique enough, you can repost the same unique part of the game that attracts attention in different 'angles'. The same core loop with different weapons being used...
- Do not care about being cringe. Do not care about showing your face or yourself. You're still most likely irrelevant in the grand scheme of things...

### If you have a demo
- Add an utm_source reference in your demo that redirects to your game main page using steam's overlay (person may not be logged if open browser). ([UTM Documentation in steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/utm_analytics)). Remember to test your UTM link inside Marketing > UTM.
- Add a popup when quitting asking for the person to wishlist (thank the person for playing, add a picture of the team, of your cat... Something to show there are humans behind the game);
